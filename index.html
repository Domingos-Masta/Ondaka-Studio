Everything in one place

A focused pre-production workflow

From the first idea to a teleprompter-ready script — without jumping between tools.

Plan with scenes & blocks

Structure every video from hook to CTA. Group related scenes into color-coded blocks and reorder freely to test your narrative flow.

Rich script editor

Write in a distraction-free editor with per-scene metadata, lockable content, and a Google-Docs-style reading column built for long-form scripts.

Timing & pacing

Set a speaking WPM, watch live word counts and estimates per scene, and fit your script to the timeline — or the timeline to your script.

Built-in teleprompter

Present in sequence, timed, or continuous scrolling mode. Mirror the text, tune font size and speed, and keep perfect pace while you record.

Optional AI assistant

Generate scripts from a title, rewrite or expand selected text, and import TXT/DOCX — powered by your own key from OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek or Anthropic.

Export & share

Export timed scene plans and CSV breakdowns, generate YouTube chapter lists, and hand a production-ready plan straight to your edit.

How it works

From outline to on-camera in four steps

Outline

Break the video into hook, intro, points, demo, transition and CTA scenes — organized into blocks.

Write

Draft each scene in the editor with live word counts, and let AI help refine the wording when you want it.

Time

Set your speaking pace and watch estimated durations update. Fit the script to your target length.

Present & export

Rehearse with the teleprompter, then export a timed plan and chapters for the edit.

Bring your own key

AI when you want it, private by default
